Research

Energy-aware decisions for dual-UAV RIS-assisted SWIPT.

Under Prof. Wenson Chang at the Intelligent Communication Lab, my M.S. research studies Voronoi-based user partitioning and DRL resource allocation in dual-UAV RIS-assisted SWIPT networks. The decision space includes time-switching, power allocation, RIS phase-shift, and energy-harvesting efficiency.

Plan

Energy-aware Voronoi partitioning assigns users to UAV service regions.

Control

DRL learns time, power, and RIS phase decisions under dynamic wireless states.

Evaluate

Measure energy-harvesting efficiency, throughput, and service quality tradeoffs.
