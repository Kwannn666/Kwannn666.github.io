03 / Master's Thesis

Dual-UAV RIS-Assisted SWIPT Networks

Voronoi-based energy-aware user partitioning and DRL resource allocation.

NCKU · Intelligent Communication Lab · 2024–2026

Energy-aware decisions in dynamic wireless networks.

My M.S. research under Prof. Wenson Chang studies how to partition users and allocate resources in dual-UAV RIS-assisted SWIPT networks.

The decision space covers time-switching, power allocation, and RIS phase-shift control, with energy-harvesting efficiency as a central objective.

Deep Reinforcement LearningVoronoi PartitioningRISSWIPT
01 / PARTITION

Plan service regions

Energy-aware Voronoi partitioning assigns users to UAV service regions.

02 / OPTIMIZE

Learn resource decisions

DRL learns time-switching, power allocation, and RIS phase decisions under dynamic wireless states.

03 / EVALUATE

Examine system tradeoffs

Study energy-harvesting efficiency, throughput, and service quality across resource-allocation decisions.
